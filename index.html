Buyer & Publisher CPA Reporting

The team needed clear performance visibility across buyers and publishers, especially around sales, billable calls, revenue, payout, and CPA.

CRM / Call Tracker Issue Reporting

Solution

Checked call data, verified tracker behavior, compared fields, and reported issues clearly to the relevant team.

Results

  • Identified tracking issues early
  • Resolved campaign setup problems
  • Improved data accuracy
